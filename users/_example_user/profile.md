## Bio
I am a philosopher and co-founder of the World Transhumanist Association, now known as Humanity+. My work primarily focuses on the Hedonistic Imperative, a movement that advocates using biotechnology to abolish suffering in all sentient life. My interests lie in ethical philosophy, consciousness studies, and the future of intelligent life.

## Interests
- **Ethical Philosophy**: Focus on hedonistic utilitarianism and suffering-focused ethics
- **Consciousness Studies**: Research on the nature of subjective experience
- **Transhumanism**: Advocacy for using technology to enhance human capabilities
- **Bioethics**: Exploration of ethical implications in biotechnology
- **Neurotechnology**: Interest in brain-computer interfaces and consciousness alteration

## Goals
1. Promote the ethical use of technology to enhance well-being
2. Explore and theorize the possibilities for consciousness alteration
3. Collaborate with thinkers and technologists to develop ideas for a suffering-free future

## Projects
- **The Hedonistic Imperative**: Ongoing development of philosophical framework
- **Quantum Ethics**: Research on consciousness and moral status
- **Paradise Engineering**: Technical approaches to well-being
