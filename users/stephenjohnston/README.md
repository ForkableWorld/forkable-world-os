# User Profile: Stephen Johnston

## Bio
Fellow, Healthy Longevity at SOMPO Digital Lab and Managing Director of Fordcastle, an impact-focused consultancy. Co-founder of Aging2.0 (acquired in 2022) and co-author of Longevity Ecosystems (MIT, forthcoming). Curates the Looking Forward newsletter. Studied Economics at Cambridge University and holds an MBA from Harvard Business School, where he was a Fulbright Scholar.
